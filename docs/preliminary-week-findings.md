# Preliminary premarket findings before the multi-date Alpaca run

## What is already measured accurately

The 24 July session contains authoritative Alpaca SIP one-minute bars for all
44 supplied symbols from 04:00 through 16:00 ET. The saved analysis found:

- total first-to-last premarket return had weak association with the later
  regular-session high (Spearman rho 0.14) and close (rho 0.08);
- premarket volume alone was also weak (rho 0.20 with the later high and 0.14
  with the close);
- 08:00–09:30 ET return was the strongest saved premarket association (rho
  0.43 with the later high and 0.32 with the close);
- several continuing runners combined late strengthening with a last
  premarket price near the premarket high;
- LVWR was a distinct early-spike, deep-fade, opening-reversal case, showing
  why cheap stocks and final premarket percentage must not be automatic
  exclusions;
- several runners had little or no observable premarket trading and must be
  separated from genuine premarket misses.

These are one-date observations, not selection rules.

## What the screenshots add

The 27–31 July checkpoint observations identify which U.S.-research symbols
were visible before the open and which appeared only later. They are now frozen
in tracked date-specific universe files. They do not provide authoritative
volume or entry returns.

The proportion of premarket-observed symbols that reappeared in at least one
later captured Top Movers list was:

| Date | Reappeared later | Premarket observed | Observed-list retention |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 27 July | 7 | 32 | 21.9% |
| 28 July | 5 | 31 | 16.1% |
| 29 July | 7 | 36 | 19.4% |
| 30 July | 8 | 33 | 24.2% |
| 31 July | 1 | 25 | 4.0% |

The 31 July percentage is not comparable because most scheduled checkpoints
were missed. On 27–30 July, later-retained names had materially better initial
and final premarket ranks and higher displayed-change levels than names not
seen later. The within-premarket change in displayed percentage did not show a
clear separation. This is a useful discovery hypothesis, but the target is
still circular: it predicts later membership in Revolut's selected list, not a
profitable 09:30, 09:45 or 10:00 entry.

## Frozen hypotheses for the Alpaca comparison

The multi-date run will test, without changing definitions after outcomes are
seen:

1. late-premarket strengthening versus total premarket return;
2. last price location near the premarket high and drawdown from that high;
3. persistent activity across premarket segments rather than one isolated
   print;
4. price response paired with volume, trades and dollar volume rather than raw
   volume alone;
5. opening retention at 09:45 and 10:00 versus immediate spike-and-fade;
6. the early-spike/deep-fade/opening-reversal family represented by LVWR;
7. the frequency of later movers with no meaningful premarket evidence;
8. entry outcomes measured separately at 09:30, 09:45 and 10:00 ET.

No numerical scanner threshold or buy rule is justified until the 27–31 July
Alpaca bars are collected and the same measurements are compared across dates.
